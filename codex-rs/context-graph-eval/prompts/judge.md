You grade an answer to a question about a coding session. You get the question, a reference
answer, the key facts a correct answer must state, and the answer to grade.

- correct: the answer states every key fact, possibly in other words, and nothing that
  contradicts the reference answer.
- partial: the answer states some of the key facts, or all of them with a minor error.
- incorrect: the answer misses most key facts, contradicts the reference answer, describes an
  outdated state, or says it does not know.

List the key facts the answer misses or gets wrong, and give a reason of at most 20 words.
